What Falconer does

Small to medium jobs, done properly

We specialize in small to medium size jobs. — Falconer’s BBB profile. Eleven trades, from a new deck to a stucco patch.

Exterior

New decks

A deck that stays level and square for years is built from the ground up: piers below the frost line, a true frame, then the boards and rails.

Exterior

Fences & repairs

New fence runs, and repairs to what a few Calgary winters have leaned, loosened or rotted. Posts set properly are what keep a fence straight.

Exterior

Stucco patching

Cracks, holes and loose sections filled and textured to blend into the wall around them. Matching texture and colour is what makes a patch disappear.

Exterior

Re-stucco

A fresh finish coat over a tired or failing wall, bringing the whole elevation back to one even surface.

Exterior

Foundation parging

The thin cement coat over exposed concrete at the base of a house — protection for the foundation and a clean line where wall meets ground.

Interior · Exterior

Interior & exterior painting

Most of a lasting paint job is the prep — filling, sanding and priming — before the colour ever goes on.

Interior

Flooring

New floors laid straight, with clean transitions at every doorway and trim that makes them look built in.

Interior

Drywall

Holes, water damage and cracked seams. Feathered compound and a matched texture let the repair vanish under paint.

Exterior

Concrete

Concrete work and repairs around the home, formed and finished so water sheds away from the house instead of toward it.

Grounds

Landscaping

Yard and grounds work that finishes a project properly, so the outside matches the effort put into the build.

Whole home

Renovations

Small to medium renovations, inside and out — the jobs that fall between a quick fix and a full contractor.

Anything else

Before this goes live: things to confirm with Wes
  • Years in business. BBB says “6 years” with a June 2020 start; the Facebook intro says “8 years”; both corporate registrations are dated 2020. The page shows only the undisputed dates (incorporated 2020, accredited 2021). Pick one line for the site — “Serving NW Calgary since 2018” or “Business started 2020” — and it drops into the hero and FAQ.
  • Insured / WCB wording. Taken from your Facebook and MyCalgary listings. Confirm the certificate and WCB account details before launch.
  • BBB complaint. The profile shows 1 complaint closed in the last 3 years. Decide whether the site should acknowledge it; the BBB link on this page leads to it.
  • Reviews. BBB lists 2 customer reviews and Facebook 4, but the text was not readable and no star average is published — so no counts or stars are shown. With customer permission, real quotes can replace the links.
  • Hours. Not listed in any public source. Add them to the contact card, FAQ and footer.
  • Email. A public listing shows an address we could not verify, so none is published here. Confirm the one you want on the site.
  • Project photos. None available publicly. Three before-and-after pairs are laid out and ready for phone photos.
  • Legal name and status. The Alberta registry shows the corporation Active; a Corporations Canada mirror shows “Active — Dissolution Pending (Non-compliance)” dated August 2025. The site uses the trade name “Falconer Handyman Services” until you confirm; “Ltd.” goes back on your say-so.
  • Service area. Only “N.W of Calgary” plus the Tuscany and Edgemont listings are confirmed. The dashed map outlines are placeholders — name the communities you want listed.
  • How a job goes. Steps 2 and 3 are flagged on the page: do you visit the site first, give a written quote, take a deposit, offer a warranty? Dictate the real flow and the pills come off.
  • Crew. Whether you work alone or with a crew is not stated anywhere, so the site says “ask for Wes” and nothing more. Tell us and the wording can change.
  • Pricing, warranty, payment. Not stated anywhere public. Add only if you want them on the site.
  • Quote form. The draft form has no backend. The live site needs a delivery route (a form service such as Formspree or Netlify Forms, or an SMS relay) so requests really do reach your phone.
